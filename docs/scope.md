# Scope

The signed scope document is the authority. This is the working summary.
If the two disagree, the signed document wins and this file is wrong.

## Pages

| Route | Purpose | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `/` | Home. The full argument, then convert | Not started |
| `/solutions` | One page, three tiers | Not started |
| `/insights` | Blog, video and the education library | Not started |
| `/team` | About and the people | Not started |
| `/contact` | Contact and lead capture | Not started |
| `/design` | Internal. Five design directions for selection | Built, delete after selection |

Education is entirely inside Insights. There is no separate education hub and
no page called "The Model".

## Home page order

Subject to change as content lands, but this is the agreed shape:

1. Hero. Background video, position statement on top, CTAs
2. The three rings animation: team, individual, enterprise
3. What the evidence says
4. Our process: Prepare, Launch, Discover, Awareness, Belonging, Action
5. Ways to engage: the three tiers
6. Proof: case studies and testimonials
7. Partners and client logos
8. Insights teaser
9. Lead capture

"Get Started" is a CTA, not a nav item, and the starting point is a section on
the home page rather than a standalone landing page.

## Explicitly out of scope

Not "later". Not in this engagement.

- User accounts, login, authentication
- Payments, Stripe, checkout
- Client dashboard or portal
- Storing assessment results
- TeamQ or any validated instrument
- Persona-driven re-rendering of the homepage

Persona routing, if it happens at all, recommends a tier and surfaces relevant
blocks. It does not rebuild the page per persona.

## The qualification quiz

Short, free, no gating. Its job is to route a visitor to a tier and capture an
email. It is explicitly not SAMUH's validated instrument and must never be
presented as one. See hard rule 6 in `CLAUDE.md`.

## Scope risk

The scope is nine pages of detail and the term is three months solo. The
following are the first things to cut if the timeline tightens, in this order:

1. The interactive data tool
2. Illustrated metaphor cards
3. Insights migration from the existing site

Raise this before it becomes a launch-week conversation.
