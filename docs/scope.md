## Get Started: the intake assessment

One person, usually a team leader, reflects on how their team scores across the
Sapien Labs team environment factors.

- Modelled on the Noom quiz. Questions interlaced with data insights and
  testimonials so the visitor falls into it rather than feeling qualified
- Value lands early. First insight before the first ask
- Free and ungated. No email to start, no email to see the result
- Email or booking offered after the result, to save or share it
- Runs on the SAMUH site. Sending visitors to Sapien Labs' system loses them

Output: result on screen, shareable link with correct preview cards, tagged
lead record if an email is given, tracked conversion event.

**This is not TeamQ.** Separate tool, own logic, must not share the TeamQ
algorithm and must not be presented as the validated assessment.

**Open:** which factors are used and how the estimate is calculated. SAMUH owns
the logic. Design proceeds once the question set is fixed.

| Tier | Description | Conversion |
|---|---|---|

| Supported | Self-guided plus periodic support calls | Talk to us |

One page, same structure each: what it is, who it is for, what you get. A
comparison table underneath.

The team process is the methodology across all three tiers. Organization-wide
engagements layer on top of it, not beside it.

**Language rule:** a multi-team organization, not an enterprise. Teams stay
central in every sentence.

## Positioning

Not settled. Recorded so design does not get ahead of it.

Working value proposition: high performance without the cost to people.
Sustained performance where people thriving and results thriving reinforce each
other rather than trading off.

The risk runs both ways. Copy that skews to performance metrics reads cold.
Copy that skews to wellbeing reads soft. Both have to hold at once.

The "leaking performance" framing is useful but broad. On its own it does not
point at team environment as the cause, which is the actual claim.

**Open:** final hero wording and the research claim. Owner is SAMUH. Needed
before homepage sections 2 and 6 are designed. The site and Daniel's hero video
script have to land on the same line.

## Insights

The education layer and the main organic acquisition channel. CMS driven so
SAMUH publishes without a developer.

- Index: foundations, articles, video and research in one filterable feed
- Topic hubs: capacity, team practices, rituals, measurement
- Research gets distinct treatment. The Sapien Labs report does not sit at blog
  weight
- One detail template for written and video. Video embedded from YouTube or
  Vimeo, never self hosted
- Author, date, read or watch time, topic tags, related items, transcript where
  there is video
- SAMUH edits: foundations, insights, team, testimonials, case studies,
  solution copy, site settings. Recorded training at handover

**Open:** existing WordPress articles and the Sapien Labs report. Migrate all,
curate, or leave. Redirects built either way.

## Out of scope

Listed in the scope document as the platform build, priced separately:

- TeamQ and OrgQ assessment engine
- User accounts and authentication
- Client dashboard
- Stripe and self serve purchase
- Content gating, free against paid
- Assessment history and benchmarking

The site is built to extend into these. The intake assessment becomes the front
door to TeamQ, the content model holds gated content, and the lead records
become the first accounts. Building in a way that forecloses that is a mistake
even though none of it ships now.

## Scope risk

Large scope, three months, one person. First things to cut if the timeline
tightens, in this order:

1. Interactive data tool
2. Illustrated metaphor cards
3. Insights migration from WordPress

Raise it before it becomes a launch-week conversation.
