## The thesis

Performance is a property of teams, not individuals. SAMUH turns organisational
research into a set of practices a team can actually run inside a normal
working week.

Content spine, in the order the argument is made:

1. The performance equation
2. Teams as the central point of impact
3. What the evidence says (Sapien capacity factors)
4. Rituals as the vehicle for change
5. The six-stage process: Prepare, Launch, Discover, Awareness, Belonging, Action

## Audience

Originally CHROs and founders. Widened to include team leads, store managers
and small high-growth startups. The site has to work for someone who has never
heard the argument before, not just someone already sold on it.

## What success looks like

A visitor who arrived cold understands the argument, believes the evidence,
and either takes the qualification quiz or contacts the sales team. That is the
whole funnel. There is nothing after the handoff.
