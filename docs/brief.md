## The thesis

High performance without the cost to people. Sustained performance where people
thriving and results thriving reinforce each other rather than trading off.

The underlying claim: teams leak performance through their environment, not
through effort. Team environment is the cause, which is more specific and more
useful than "performance is leaking".

## Audience

CHROs and founders originally, widened to team leads, store managers and small
high-growth startups. The site has to land for someone who has never heard the
argument, not only someone already sold.

Buyers include Fortune 500 companies. That sets the credibility floor.

## Tone

The client does not want to look corporate. Their own description of what they
want, agreed in conversation: an onboarding process for new clients, but on
mushrooms.

Read that as playful, organic, a little psychedelic, and confident enough to
not look like every other consultancy. Not as childish, and not as unserious.
The practical split:

- **Looseness** lives in the background treatment, the motion, the illustration
  and the gap between hairline and heavy stroke
- **Discipline** lives in the typography, the spacing and the restraint of
  using one loud colour at a time

That split is what lets it read as distinctive to a founder and as credible to
a Fortune 500 procurement team looking at the same page.

either completes the intake assessment or books a call. The handoff to the
sales team is the end of the site's job.
