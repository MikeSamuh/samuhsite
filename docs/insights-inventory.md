## What this means for the Insights build

The scope wants Insights to be CMS driven, with one template for written and
video posts, video embedded from YouTube or Vimeo, and a transcript wherever
there is video.

**1. Find out where these videos already live.** The banner assets say there
was a YouTube channel. If the eighteen episodes are already on it, we embed
and we are done. If not, someone has to upload 20GB to YouTube or Vimeo before
any of this can be on the site, because the scope rules out self-hosting.

**2. Transcripts do not exist here.** The scope requires one per video. That is
18 episodes plus the keynotes and the Sapien Labs pieces. Budget it, or agree
that launch ships with fewer videos.

**3. Curate before migrating.** Suggested launch set: episodes 11 to 18, the two
Sapien Labs conversations, the Leaders Lounge keynote, and the HBR piece. That
is twelve items and every one is on thesis. Episodes 1 to 9 are a second pass
with Rahul. Episode 10 is out.

**4. Three episodes are incomplete.** 5 has no horizontal cut. 6 and 7 have
thumbnails and nothing else. Ask whether the videos exist or the episodes were
never published.

**5. Titles for 6 and 7 are unknown.** Thumbnails only. Rahul will know.

**6. The HBR material is the strongest written credibility on offer.** A
published HBR piece, co-authored with Sapien Labs, sits naturally in the
research tier of Insights. Confirm publication status before it goes on the
site as "as seen in HBR".
