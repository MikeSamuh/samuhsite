# Content

## How copy flows

1. Client writes in Google Docs, one doc per page, in `04 Content/Page Copy`
2. Headings in the doc match the section names in `docs/scope.md`
3. We pull it into the build when the section is being built
4. Once a CMS exists, copy moves there and the Docs become the archive

We do not accept copy as .docx attachments, inside a deck, or in the body of an
email. That is how retyping and version drift happen.

## Placeholder discipline

Anything not yet supplied by the client is written as:

```
TODO(content): one line saying what is needed and who owns it
```

Visible in the UI during development. Never a plausible-looking invented
value. See hard rule 1 in `CLAUDE.md`.

This matters most for numbers. Sample sizes, percentages, years of research
and case study outcomes must come from SAMUH in writing. A fabricated figure
that reaches a client deck is unrecoverable.

## What we cannot state yet

- Any sample size or research figure
- "Two decades of research" or any specific number of years
- Named clients
- Testimonial attributions
- The number of team practices

The sample copy on `/design` uses the confirmed items only. Do not copy the
placeholder caption `FIG. 01 · SAPIEN CAPACITY FACTORS · N = 407,000` into
production. It exists to show the mono face and the number is not verified.

## Voice

Plain, declarative, unhurried. The argument is strong enough that it does not
need selling language. No exclamation marks, no "unlock", no "transform your
organisation". No em dashes.
