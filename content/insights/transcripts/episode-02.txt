saw the biggest improvement with every older age of smartphone acquisition.
And finally, four, suicidal thoughts and tendencies of feeling aggression towards the others
also consistently improved with every older year in which people got to acquire their smartphones.
Interestingly, the age of smartphone ownership was lower in the developed world.
North America, Europe, and Australia, kids were getting, on average,
a smartphone between 11.5 to 12.5 years,
whereas in places like Africa or South Asia,
the average age of first smartphone ownership was between 16 and 17.
