We seem to be living in this tremendously polarizing and chaotic world which is marked
by so much human conflict, a planet in distress, a mental well-being crisis of proportions we've
never seen before. And through all of this we start to find solutions and answers in the outside
world. For example in technology thinking that we could feel better with the next app or more
connected with the next social media platform or more gratified with more likes and followers.
Or we could have this wondrous life in the metaverse and maybe Gen.ai could have all the answers
to these questions that we seek. But our answers may not lie in the next shiniest object or the next
development in technology. In fact we may be well served by learning lessons and gleaning wisdom
through the course of humanity because this is not the first time the world has been like this.
Through the course of human history we've seen devastating calamities and great wars and yet the human spirit has soared
and our ancestors through generations captured their learnings in scriptures and texts in an effort to pass down
for those that are to follow. In fact even today there are people that are living their lives in synchronicity
with universal laws of nature and showing us the way to lead happy and healthy lives.
Hector Garcia and Francesc Mirales set out to study the longest living people on earth.
Their explorations took them to Ogimi, a village in Okinawa in Japan which boasts the highest life expectancy
in the world. They talked to scores of centenarians, people that lived more than a hundred years
and lived amongst them and what they found was that the secret to a happy and long life lay in staying active,
eating in a balanced and mindful way, persevering through difficulties, doing low intensity exercises,
reducing stress, reducing stress, sleeping and crucially nurturing friendships throughout their lives.
They call it Ikigai, the reason to wake up each morning. May we all find our Ikigai. May we all take inspiration
from thousands and thousands of years of human history and the wisdom that our ancestors have passed on to us
and perhaps get fascinated by what that might offer us rather than just the next new shiny thing.
