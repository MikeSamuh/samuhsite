## Summary

Rahul introduces himself for viewers who are new to the series. He spent nearly three decades in corporate life, much of it leading human resources for large global workforces across India, Asia and the US. In 2007 an asthma attack led to a cardiac arrest that nearly killed him, which he attributes to years of neglect, overwork and a poor lifestyle, and which taught him that caring for mind and body is a condition of performing at a high level. Eighteen months before this video he left his job to study the human mind, body, heart and spirit with experts across disciplines, and joined the board of Sapien Labs, whose scientists have built the largest database of mental wellbeing in the world and are seeing declines that deepen with each younger generation. The series is his way of passing along what he learns, drawn from validated research and enduring wisdom, kept short for busy people.

## Key points

- Nearly three decades in HR leadership across India, Asia and the US
- A cardiac arrest in 2007, which he attributes to overwork and lifestyle
- Taking care of mind and body is a condition of sustained high performance
- He left his role to become a student again and joined the board of Sapien Labs
- The videos pass along what he learns, from validated research and enduring wisdom
