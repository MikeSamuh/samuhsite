## Summary

Rahul opens the series with his own story. He went into human resources three decades ago because he saw it as the one function in an organisation that could help people thrive. Years of working every hour of the day ended in a cardiac arrest following an asthma attack, and coming out of it he asked again why he was here. The answer was the same, to help people thrive, and he could not do that without thriving himself. He later left a large HR leadership role to dedicate himself to that purpose, and this series is his way of raising awareness with research and facts and opening a dialogue on solutions that move the needle.

## Key points

- He chose HR because it was the function that could help people thrive
- Overwork led to a near-death experience, and that forced the question of purpose
- He could not help others thrive without taking care of his own wellness first
- He walked away from a large, well-paid role because it no longer matched that purpose
- The series aims to raise awareness with research and start a dialogue on solutions that work
